## 1. What's Good About GimnApp (Sell These Points)

Use these as the core selling points in every piece of content. Lead with outcomes, not features.

### 🏆 Unique Selling Points
- **First-of-its-kind in Serbia** — the only official student parliament digital platform of its kind in a Serbian gymnasium. You're not joining an app; you're making history.

- **Channels** — topic-based group chat spaces inside the app.
- **Gimnazija Feed** — the school's own social network. Post, like, comment — without Instagram's algorithm deciding who sees your stuff.

## 3. Brand Voice & Messaging Rules

### Voice attributes
- **Kao drug iz razreda** — smart friend, not a teacher. Never preachy.
- Short sentences. Real talk. Zero corporate speak.
- Confident, slightly playful. Memes allowed when native, never forced.
- Write in Serbian (latinica), casual "ti" form always.

### Hard rules
- ❌ No fabricated stats ("1000 učenika već koristi!" unless true)
- ✅ Specificity beats hype ("Glasaj na 3 predloga do petka" > "Učestvuj!")
- ✅ One idea per post. One CTA per post.
- ❌ Never mock teachers/school in content — we're official, not a meme page attacking anyone

### Copy formula for every post
```
Hook (pattern interrupt, ≤7 words)
→ Value (what you get)
→ Proof/visual (show the actual app)
→ CTA (one action)
```

## 5. Channel Strategy

### Instagram = home base (owned-ish)
- **Bio:** `Zvanična aplikacija Učeničkog parlamenta ŠG 🎓 | Glasaj, pitaj, prati | Besplatno ↓`
- **Highlights:** Kako radi · Glasanje · AI · Pitanja · Pobednici (class comp)
- Frequency: 1 feed post/reel daily during launch, 5+ stories daily
- Links in bio only (link kills reach in body); say "link u bio-u"

### TikTok = reach engine
- Native, unpolished > produced. Even with pro gear, keep cuts fast and raw-feeling.
- 1–2 posts/day. Hook in first 1.5s. Vertical 9:16. Trending sounds where possible.
- Formats that work for school accounts:
  1. POV skits ("POV: direktor kaže da glasamo o pravilima")
  2. Screen recordings with voiceover (feature demos)
  3. Street interviews ("Šta bi promenio u školi?" → "Sad možeš da glasate o tome")
  4. AI demo reveals
  5. Countdown/mystery teasers
- Reply to top comments WITH video replies (algorithm fuel)

### Borrowed credibility
- 2–3 parliament members appear on camera by name/face → trust transfer
- One teacher or the director says one supportive sentence on camera (massive legitimacy)
- Local angle: pitch a short piece to Šabačke lokalne novine/portali ("Gimnazija prva u Srbiji uvodi digitalni parlament") — free PR, parents see it too

## 7. Growth Loops (Referral + Viral)

### Loop 1 — Word of mouth (primary, expect K≈0.3)
Trigger: friend shows friend the app at break. Amplifier: make signup < 60s (Google/Clerk one-tap). Measure: % of new users from same class clusters.

### Loop 2 — Class competition (launch window)
- Prize: pizza party for winning class (fund via parliament budget or local sponsor who gets a shoutout)
- Mechanic: signup linked to class selection at onboarding (add if missing) OR manual tally via referral codes `3a`, `3b`...
- Public leaderboard posted weekly in stories + physical poster in hallway
- Psychology: intergroup rivalry is the strongest adoption lever in schools

### Loop 3 — Easter egg hunt (evergreen curiosity engine)
- `67.gimnapp.me` exists. Don't explain it. Ever. Add more hidden subdomains over time (`ai.gimnapp.me` is public but can gain hidden features).
- Drop occasional hints in stories: "onima koji znaju za 67 — vi ste pravi jedini"
- Reward discoverers with a badge/role in app if feasible

### Loop 4 — Referral rewards (post-launch, simple version)
- Double-sided, non-cash: referrer gets "Legendarni osnivači" OG badge/badge role + shoutout; new user gets nothing extra (app is already free — don't overcomplicate)
- Tiered recognition (Morning Brew model): 3 invites = shoutout story · 10 = OG badge · 25 = "Legenda" status + pick next poll question
- Keep fraud-safe: requires real activity before counting (they already have Clerk auth = verified emails)

## 12. Risks & Contingencies

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| App feels empty early | Seeded content mandatory before reveal (Phase 0) |
| Cringe factor / rejection by cool kids | Founding users chosen for social capital, not just enthusiasm; humor-forward content, zero "official" stiffness |
| Signup friction kills conversion | Test Google one-tap login flow yourself on school wifi beforehand |
| Vote with no real consequence | Only announce votes the parliament commits to acting on; publish outcomes |
| Moderation incident in feed/channels | ADMIN tools exist — assign 2 moderators for launch weeks, response protocol: hide → DM → escalate |
| Privacy concerns from parents/school | Privatnost + Uslovi pages exist; link them in bio highlight; GDPR-friendly Clerk auth |
| Content burnout after week 2 | Batch Sundays, reuse templates, cut to 3 posts/week minimum viable rhythm |
